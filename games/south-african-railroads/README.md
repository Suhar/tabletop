# South African Railroads

`@tabletop/south-african-railroads` is the deterministic logic package for South African Railroads, a share-auction railroad game by John Bohrer, published by Winsome Games, for 3–6 players. Its companion Game Client package is `@tabletop/south-african-railroads-ui`.

## Rules

The public `Definition` covers the initial offering, the Construct Track, Offer Stock, Develop Settlements and Pay Dividends actions, the opening of the ZASM at Johannesburg, external connections with their special dividends, and the final payoff after the sixth dividend. Where the rulebook is silent or ambiguous, the choices made are recorded in [rules interpretations](docs/rules-interpretations.md), each backed by a test.
